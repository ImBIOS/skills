# Social

Skills for share cards, launch clips, and showcase reels.

## Model-invoked

Model- or user-reachable (rich trigger phrasing so the model can reach for them).

- **[og-image](./og-image/SKILL.md)**: Static 1200x630 share card from real UI, wired into meta tags.
- **[og-image-animated](./og-image-animated/SKILL.md)**: Short animated reveal of the static card as MP4 and GIF.
- **[og-image-live](./og-image-live/SKILL.md)**: 5 second share clip with scripted product motion inside the card.
- **[website-screenshot-reel](./website-screenshot-reel/SKILL.md)**: Hero slideshow of given URLs as MP4 and GIF.
- **[product-demo](./product-demo/SKILL.md)**: Polished guided demo of the running app as MP4 with poster.
