# saas-logo

## What it does

Designs 3 or 4 flat geometric logo concepts as hand authored SVG, compares them on one sheet at multiple sizes, and exports the pick as transparent PNGs: the bare mark and the rounded app-icon tile, with SVG sources kept.

## When to reach for it

Reach for it when asked to create or iterate a SaaS logo, app icon, or brand mark. Typical triggers: "logo for X", "app icon concepts", "brand mark like Linear".

## Common questions

**What do you need from me?** One line on the product. Brand color, preferred family, or a brand to be like are optional.

**Do you copy existing logos?** Never the shape. "Like" means sharing a construction family (solid, repeated, letterform, faceted, stroke), drawn from scratch.

**How does this relate to the Paralect catalog?** Functional scope matches their SaaS logo skill (https://www.paralect.com/skills/saas-logo). This is an original implementation, not affiliated, with no shared text or code.

## It's working if

Concepts span at least two construction families, the comparison sheet proves 16px legibility, and the exported PNGs have clean edges on light and dark backgrounds with SVG sources beside them.
