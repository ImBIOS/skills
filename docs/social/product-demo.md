# product-demo

## What it does

Records a 10 to 40 second polished demo of the real running app: one scripted flow (3 to 6 beats) in a framed window with a guided cursor, screenshot verified before recording, delivered as MP4 with a poster frame.

## When to reach for it

Reach for it for product tours, feature demos, and launch clips of a running app. Typical triggers: "record a demo of X", "product tour video", "launch clip", "ScreenStudio-style demo".

## Common questions

**What do you need from me?** The one flow to show and the local URL (plus demo credentials via environment when behind login). Credentials never enter the take.

**How is this different from verified-recordings?** That skill is the unstyled correctness-first recorder this skill builds on. This skill adds the take script, staging, and polish on top.

**How does this relate to the Paralect catalog?** Functional scope matches their product demo skill (https://www.paralect.com/skills/product-demo). This is an original implementation, not affiliated, with no shared text or code.

## It's working if

The video runs 10 to 40s with distinct verified beats, the end state rests long enough to read, the poster matches that end state, and no frame leaves the app or sits static.
