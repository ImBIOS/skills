# website-screenshot-reel

## What it does

Turns a URL list into a short slideshow of their hero sections: 2x captures with banners dismissed, 1.2s holds with quick crossfades, delivered as MP4 plus GIF.

## When to reach for it

Reach for it for screenshot reels, hero slideshows, or multi-site showcases. Typical triggers: "screenshot reel of these sites", "hero slideshow", "compare these homepages as a clip".

## Common questions

**How many URLs?** 3 to 8. Fewer feels empty, more drags past attention span.

**Login pages?** They capture the login screen. Confirm that is what the caller wants before proceeding.

**How does this relate to the Paralect catalog?** Functional scope matches their screenshot reel skill (https://www.paralect.com/skills/website-screenshot-reel). This is an original implementation, not affiliated, with no shared text or code.

## It's working if

Every URL appears once in order with no banners, text is crisp at full size, and MP4 plus GIF meet the size budgets.
