# og-image-live

## What it does

Makes a 5 second share clip where the product moves inside an otherwise fixed card: records landing, status pills cycling, a chart extending. One loopable scripted moment, delivered as MP4 plus GIF with a poster frame.

## When to reach for it

Reach for it when asked for a live-feeling share clip or motion version of the share card. Typical triggers: "live og image", "share video with product motion", "animated product moment in the card".

## Common questions

**How is this different from the animated card?** The animated card reveals static elements once. The live card holds its chrome fixed while a scripted product moment loops inside.

**How many motions per clip?** Exactly one. Two moments read as noise at feed size.

**How does this relate to the Paralect catalog?** Functional scope matches their live OG image skill (https://www.paralect.com/skills/og-image-live). This is an original implementation, not affiliated, with no shared text or code.

## It's working if

The clip is 5 seconds with one readable product moment, the loop restart is invisible, the poster shows the resting card, and both MP4 and GIF play cleanly at feed size.
